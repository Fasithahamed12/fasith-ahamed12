PIVL

Persistent Instruction Verification Loop is a model-agnostic control layer for long-running AI agents. It keeps standing instructions and prior failures outside ordinary conversational context, then verifies important actions before they execute.

How PIVL works

Standing instructions ↓ Persistent rule registry ↓ Route relevant constraints ↓ Model proposes action ↓ Pre-action verifier fail ↙ ↘ pass Failure memory Execute ↓ ↓ Prevention rule Post-check ↘ ↙ Next action

Research status: this page demonstrates the enforcement mechanism. It does not claim that PIVL has already improved GPT, Claude, Gemini, or any other model in controlled A/B testing.

Research hypothesis

Long-horizon agent reliability may improve when binding instructions and prior violations are maintained in persistent external state, relevant rules are retrieved at action time, objectively testable rules are compiled into deterministic predicates, and important actions are blocked until applicable constraints pass verification.
